Legacy Review — Upgrade Candidates for FIE

Synthesis of four deep reviews (QAE, FVE, QE v2, MSIL + shared). Each card is self-contained — feed one at a time.

Meta-insight. Legacy was deterministic, contract-first, governance-heavy (no LLM in the answer path); ours is LLM-narrating with lighter governance. Its value to us is its trust / reconciliation / citation spine, not its answer generation. Plan: graft the governance spine onto our narration.

Convergence signal. Three independently-built engines surfaced the same three ideas — claim-type-scoped authority (not flat reliability), min-weakest-link confidence, and surface-don't-resolve divergence. That convergence = real architectural truths, not one team's taste.

Tier 1 — Adopt (high value, self-contained, closes known gaps)

Scale-normalize before comparing

Tier 1MSIL · financial_year_consolidator.py:879 · MetricValuedrop-in

Detects ×10³/×10⁶/×10⁹ disagreements within a ~5% ratio tolerance and converts to canonical absolute PKR before any tolerance check.

Fixes: our ad-hoc "Rs thousand vs Rs million vs absolute" handling and the false internal_vs_external conflicts it causes.

Why now: highest value, lowest risk — the code already exists and has minimal deps.

Claim-level citation enforcement + precision-capping

Tier 1QE · v2_citation_enforcer.pyself-contained (adapter)

Upgrades our numeric-only guard to a per-claim gate: every shipped claim must cite at a precision its provenance supports (CELL>PAGE>DATE>REF); uncitable claims are dropped; if all drop → clean INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE off-ramp. Enforced twice (model boundary + enforcer).

Fixes: "no citation, no claim" currently only guards numbers, not every claim.

Numeric admission role model

Tier 1FVE · numeric_admission.py + numeric_admission_gate.pyport pattern

Every external number gets a typed role (baseline | supporting | event_fact | forecast_context | non_authoritative); a validator makes "external → baseline" unconstructable. Only an audited, integrity-passed path may touch baseline math.

Fixes: structural enforcement of the trust model — far stronger than a string-level guard. Port the role enum + (source,authority,claim_type)→role policy onto our taxonomy.

Deterministic evidence ranker

Tier 1QE · v2_evidence_ranker.pyself-contained

Multi-signal score (provenance-completeness×100, authority×20, recency, corroboration, relevance) with explicit inclusion/exclusion + reason.

Fixes: our "almost no ranking" gap; feeds a confidence ceiling.

Independence / circular-evidence rejection (news)

Tier 1MSIL · evidence_comparison.py:491port pattern

Corroboration only counts sources with distinct authority classes and no shared lineage (derived_from/re_reported_from). Strength = 1 − 0.5^(n−1).

Fixes a real defect we introduced: our 8-provider failover + cosine dedup still treats 5 outlets reprinting one Reuters wire as 5 confirmations. Track lineage on news items and gate corroboration on it.

Tier 2 — High value, re-implement on our models (algorithms transfer, contracts don't)

Qualitative taxonomy → themes → coverage gate

Tier 2QAE · taxonomy JSON + theme_canonicalizer + coverage_gateJSON drop-in / port algos

Turns our flat "insights" into a structured 6-category / 27-theme taxonomy (JSON already tuned for PSX/Pakistani report language), a deterministic 4-tier canonicalizer, and — most valuable — a coverage gate distinguishing "no risk found" from "risk section never read" (e.g. "governance coverage: insufficient"). Adds three orthogonal scores (confidence / evidence-weight / materiality) and non-dilutive risk ranking.

Upgrades: risk_assessment intent and what feeds the ReasoningGraph (narrate over ~10–20 themes, not N scattered insights).

Volatility-adaptive forecast validation

Tier 2FVE · revenue_*_validation_rule.pyself-contained (drop metric hardcode)

Replaces our one-line "% above/below latest actual" with typed PASS/WARNING/FAIL: growth-band, trend-break with σ-adaptive thresholds + direction-reversal detection, and scale-plausibility — metric-agnostic, full calc trace.

Upgrades: the depth our forecast_validation intent lacks.

Claim-type-scoped authority matrix

Tier 2MSIL · models/authority.py:142self-contained

Replaces our flat per-source reliability rating with contextual trust: a broker's forecast outranks an annual report's outlook, but the audited report dominates facts. effective_rank(claim_type, authority_class) is the primitive our conflict resolver is missing.

Surface-don't-resolve divergence

Tier 2MSIL + FVE + QAE (all three)port pattern

Present both sides with an authority-weighted + chronology verdict and provenance, instead of silently picking by recency/confidence.

Upgrades: auditability of conflicts.py. (Cross-engine convergence — strong signal.)

Min-weakest-link confidence composition

Tier 2FVE · ConfidenceComposer · QAE · mapping_confidence.pyport pattern

confidence = min(rule, gate, evidence) with rationale strings and ceilings (e.g. keyword-only mapping can't claim 0.95).

Upgrades: makes High/Med/Low explainable; stops the narrated answer out-claiming its evidence. (Surfaced independently by two engines.)

Fixes: the "no CI gate" gap from the production-readiness review.

What to skip (reviewer consensus)

Leave behind

  • Multi-engine ownership / version-pin ceremony (frozen contract substrate, semver pins on every model).
  • Per-phase self-audit scaffolding (~200 lines of report-writing per service) — lift the invariants, not the scaffolding.
  • The LLM-free answer assembly — would regress our narrative quality. Take its guardrails, keep our synthesis.
  • Heavy multi-provenance unions (7 provenance types) and MVP seed registries (Lucky/Millat test data).
  • The MSIL evidence adapters — entangled with their frozen Phase contracts.

Suggested sequence

  2. T1-3 admission role model →
  3. T2-8 authority matrix →
  4. T1-2 citation enforcer →
